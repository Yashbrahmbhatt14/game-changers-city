Fighting

  • Punch three times fast for a combo; the third hit is an uppercut that knocks people down.
  • Kicks always knock people down. Some people fight back, and the rival crew always does.

Wanted level

  • Shooting near cops, knocking people out and blowing up cars raise your stars.
  • 1–2 stars: cops try to arrest you. Stand still next to one and you get BUSTED (you lose your guns).
  • 3 stars: cops shoot on sight. 4 stars: a helicopter. 5 stars: the army sends a tank.
  • Stay out of every cop's sight for about 8 seconds to drop a star, or drive into Pay N Spray (blue S on the map) to clear it for $500.

Missions at TGC HQ

  • Walk or drive into a glowing light pillar at the corners of the HQ plaza.
  • Street Race ($5,000): grab a car and hit 8 checkpoints in time.
  • Rampage ($8,000): rocket launcher provided, destroy 8 vehicles in 60 seconds.
  • Street Fight ($10,000): take down the 4 rival crew members who come for you.
  • Most Wanted ($12,000): start with 3 stars and lose the cops within 150 seconds.

Fly, sail and drive

  • Helicopters wait on the helipads at TGC HQ, the police station and the airfield. W/S fly forward and back, A/D turn, Space climbs, Shift drops. On phones: stick to fly, UP and DOWN buttons for height. You can land on rooftops.
  • The plane is at the start of the airfield runway, east of the city. Hold W to raise throttle, and once you pass about 100 km/h press Space to pull up. A/D bank and turn, Shift dives. Land gently on flat ground.
  • Speedboats are tied up at the pier on the south beach. They drive like cars on the water. Get out near the pier or beach.
  • Jumping out of a flying aircraft is allowed, but a high fall hurts.

TGC Sports City

  • Follow the road west from the city to the TGC Sports City gate. Walk into a glowing pillar outside a stadium to play.
  • Cricket (batting or bowling), Football (5-a-side) and Badminton, each with Easy, Medium and Hard. Wins pay cash.
  • Press Esc during a match to leave.

Club, cars and camera

  • TGC Club (purple D on the map): music, lights and dancers. Stand on the dance floor to dance. The juice bar gives full health and a speed boost.
  • TGC Motors (gold $ on the map) sells supercars, a helicopter and even a tank. Cars you buy can be called any time from Pause → My Garage.
  • In a vehicle, press C (or CAM on phones) to switch between chase, close and driver views.
  • On phones, cars use ◀ ▶ to steer and GAS / BRAKE pedals.

Map and extras

  • Pink T: TGC HQ · White H: hospital (you wake up here when WASTED) · Blue P: police station · Blue S: Pay N Spray.
  • 12 spinning TGC tokens are hidden in alleys and parks, $1,000 each. Find all 12 and a tank appears at HQ.
  • Floating icons on sidewalks are weapons, health and armor. They come back after a while.
  • Your cash, guns, tokens and missions save in this browser.
